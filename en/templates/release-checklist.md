# Submission and Release Checklist

Work:
Version:
Target territory/platform:
Review date:

## Content
- [ ] The author has read the complete final file
- [ ] Story promise, character arcs, and ending are complete
- [ ] Timeline, rules, character states, and terminology agree
- [ ] Every planted clue is paid off, intentionally open, or retired
- [ ] Chapter order, contents, punctuation, and formatting are consistent
- [ ] Age rating and content warnings are accurate

## Facts and originality
- [ ] Important professional facts, quotations, and sources are verified
- [ ] Title, names, organizations, and distinctive concepts passed a similarity check
- [ ] No unlicensed long quotations, lyrics, poems, images, or other assets remain
- [ ] Identifiable-person, privacy, reputation, and real-world prototype risks are handled

## AI and platform
- [ ] Current platform AI policy and submission/contract terms were read
- [ ] AI service input, output, and commercial-use terms were checked
- [ ] Required AI disclosures or labels are complete
- [ ] Required provenance labels or metadata were not removed or concealed

## Rights and files
- [ ] Author, collaborator, contractor, and asset rights are documented
- [ ] Translation, adaptation, audio, cover, and illustration rights are separately confirmed
- [ ] Comments, prompts, tracked changes, and sensitive metadata are removed
- [ ] Final filename and version are unambiguous
- [ ] Source, submission file, policy snapshot, contract, and receipt are backed up

## Final decision
- [ ] All high-risk findings are resolved or explicitly accepted by the author
- [ ] The author has approved the exact external release action

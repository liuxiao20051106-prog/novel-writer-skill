Fill this in after the draft: without looking at the text, write one line per chapter from memory, then complete it against the actual text. Chapters you can't summarize, runs of near-identical summaries, chapters with no character choice — those are what revision should attack. See [Revision Process](../references/craft-revision.md).

## How to use

1. Fill the "one-line summary" column from memory; blanks are allowed.
2. Check against the prose and fill in "change", "hook", "emotional landing".
3. Fill the "verdict" column: keep / compress / merge / rewrite / delete.
4. Reading only the verdict column gives you a revision map — structure first, sentences later.

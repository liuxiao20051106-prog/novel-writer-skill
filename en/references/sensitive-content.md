# Sensitive Content

Use this guide for violence, abuse, self-harm, suicide, sexual content, minors, hate, discrimination, trauma, addiction, illness, or other material likely to affect reader safety and platform eligibility.

## Establish purpose and boundaries

Before drafting, define why the material is necessary, whose experience is centered, the intended age rating, detail level, prohibited elements, and whether implication can achieve the same narrative goal.

Avoid glamorizing or operationalizing harmful behavior. Do not provide step-by-step instructions that increase a reader’s ability to cause harm. Sexual content involving minors is prohibited; age ambiguity must be resolved before drafting.

## Write with control

- Preserve agency and consequences; do not reduce trauma to decoration or motivation for another character.
- Avoid stereotypes that link protected traits with villainy, pathology, or incompetence.
- Distinguish a character’s harmful view from the work’s framing.
- Use specific but non-exploitative detail, especially for violence and humiliation.
- For cultural, medical, or identity-specific material, research responsibly and consider a qualified sensitivity reader.

## Review before release

Check content warnings, age rating, discoverability labels, cover and blurb signals, platform restrictions, and jurisdiction-specific requirements. Warnings should be accurate enough to help readers without revealing more than necessary.

If the author’s boundaries, character ages, consent, or intended market are unclear, pause and request a decision rather than generating the scene.

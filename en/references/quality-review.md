# Layered Quality Review

Use this guide after a scene, chapter, arc, or manuscript exists. Review from high-impact issues to surface polish so prose work is not wasted on material that will be cut.

> When review finds a concrete problem, load the matching craft guide per the SKILL.md baselines table: dragging → [Pacing Control](craft-pacing.md); falling out of the story → [Immersion Design](craft-immersion.md); weak dialogue → [Dialogue Writing](craft-dialogue.md); broken bridging → [Chapter Bridging](craft-chapter-bridging.md); machine voice → [Removing the AI Voice](craft-ai-tells.md).
>
> Quantified aid: run `python scripts/draft_diagnostics.py chapter.md` first for sentence/paragraph coefficient of variation, dialogue share, sensory density, and AI-tell density, then review layer by layer. For the round-based order, see [Revision Process](craft-revision.md).

## Review order

### 1. Promise and structure

Check whether the work delivers its genre and emotional promise, whether events form a causal chain, and whether turning points create irreversible change. Flag scenes that repeat information or do not change goal, knowledge, relationship, risk, or resources.

### 2. Character and viewpoint

Check motive, choice, consequence, knowledge boundaries, arc movement, and POV stability. Important outcomes should arise from character decisions rather than unexplained coincidence.

### 3. Scene function and pacing

Each scene needs a goal, resistance, change, and consequence. Review compression versus expansion, escalation, recovery beats, and chapter-ending momentum.

### 4. Continuity and facts

Compare the draft with the project record and source log. Mark contradictions and unverified claims; do not repair them by guessing.

### 5. Prose and dialogue

Improve clarity, rhythm, concrete detail, subtext, speaker distinction, and narrative distance. Remove redundancy, vague intensifiers, accidental repetition, and exposition that characters would not naturally say.

### 6. Proof and release risks

Check names, terminology, numbering, formatting, content warnings, privacy, originality, rights, and platform requirements.

## Make findings actionable

For each finding, provide:

- severity and exact location;
- evidence from the text or project record;
- why it harms the intended experience;
- the smallest viable fix;
- dependencies that need rechecking.

Do not average away critical failures. Use [the quality scorecard](../templates/quality-scorecard.md) for evidence-based scoring and the release checklist before publication.

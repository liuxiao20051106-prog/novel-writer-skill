# Publishing, Translation, and Adaptation

Use this guide when preparing a manuscript for submission, self-publishing, serialization, translation, audio, or screen adaptation.

## Freeze the release candidate

Assign a version, preserve the editable source and backup, and separate manuscript, metadata, cover assets, and submission package. Run the [release checklist](../templates/release-checklist.md) against the exact file that will be submitted.

## Validate the package

Confirm title, author name, synopsis, keywords, categories, age rating, content warnings, chapter order, table of contents, front and back matter, typography, links, image rights, and accessibility. Preview every target format and device class that matters.

## Verify rules and rights

Read the current platform or publisher terms directly. Confirm AI disclosure, originality, exclusivity, pricing authority, territory, language, tax or payment setup, takedown procedure, and appeal path. Keep the rule links and check date.

For translation, record the source edition, translator, glossary, cultural adaptations, and approval process. For audio or screen adaptation, specify which rights, characters, settings, sequel options, credits, approvals, and derivative assets are included.

## Authorize external actions separately

Drafting metadata or a submission package does not authorize uploading, publishing, paying fees, accepting terms, signing contracts, emailing people, or changing a live listing. Obtain explicit authorization for the exact external action.

## Preserve evidence

Archive the submitted file, cover, metadata, policy snapshot, licenses, contract, confirmation receipt, and publication URL. Record later corrections as new versions rather than silently replacing the release record.

# Copyright, Privacy, and Compliance

This is a practical risk checklist, not legal advice. Applicable law, contracts, and platform terms vary by jurisdiction and can change.

## Originality and copyright

- Do not reproduce substantial passages, lyrics, poems, scripts, paywalled text, or distinctive expression without permission.
- Do not create a close imitation of a living author’s style; translate the request into general craft traits.
- Record licenses and permissions for covers, fonts, images, translations, audio, and commissioned work.
- Similar ideas or tropes may be common, but names, characters, settings, and expression still require an originality review.
- Confirm who owns contributions from coauthors, editors, contractors, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Real people and privacy

Minimize personal data. Remove unnecessary identifiers from prompts and research notes. For identifiable living people, verify public-interest relevance, sources, consent where appropriate, and defamation or false-light risk. “Fictional” labels do not automatically remove risk when a person remains identifiable.

Never expose addresses, contact details, private records, credentials, intimate material, or confidential business information.

## AI service and platform rules

Before submission, verify current terms for input use, output rights, commercial use, disclosure, labeling, content restrictions, and appeals. Keep a dated policy snapshot or link. Do not assume one platform’s rules apply to another.

## Contract and rights review

Identify language, territory, term, exclusivity, formats, translation, audio, adaptation, sequel, character, merchandise, termination, reversion, accounting, and audit clauses. Ask a qualified professional when the stakes or uncertainty justify it.

Use [the release checklist](../templates/release-checklist.md) before any external publication. Publishing or signing remains an explicit human decision.
